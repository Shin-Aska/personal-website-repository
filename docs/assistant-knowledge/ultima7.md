# Ultima VII knowledge audit

Reviewed 2026-10-24 against the trusted local `DB` declarations, atlas adapter output and authorized records. This is a source audit, not an independent play-through. This expansion adds no external research. Existing separately cited research retains its original label and citations.

The starting adapter produces **729 records**: 76 quest records, 28 companion records, 18 equipment records, 4 bestiary records, 7 spell records, 8 reagent records, 146 question/topic records, 6 research profiles and 436 atlas/other records. The authored guide includes six main-quest stages, eight Forge of Virtue stages and fifteen side quests; nine companions, nine equipment entries, four combat summaries, seven spell summaries and eight reagent price rows. Atlas descriptions provide many additional named people, services, shrines and places. Those counts describe source inventory; they are not a claim that every conceivable question about those subjects is answerable.

The new manifest contains **104 reviewed facets**, **90 graph entities** and **52 directed evidence links**. Facets include 28 campaign, 19 Forge of Virtue, 28 side-quest and 29 reference/system access points. These are retrieval facets, not 104 independent newly discovered facts: some deliberately expose the same exact passage under an item subject and a quest/action subject. The generic source index remains responsible for broad inventory and atlas access. The curated layer makes item purposes, handoffs, alternatives, explicit conditions and consequences inspectable.

Every new `text` equals its `witness`, and every witness is an exact substring of the cited existing record after `authorize(record, 'direct')`. The authoring pass additionally checked source file and locator equality. In particular, raw guide prose that is quarantined in an authorized direct variant was not reintroduced through the new manifest. Aliases are query/access labels, not assertions that two people or objects are identical. Graph links need their own evidence fact IDs; none grants an inverse edge or player-progress claim.

## Campaign coverage

- Trinsic: stable crime scene and key, the man-with-a-hook **suspect clue**, Crown Jewel escape-vessel clue, report/quiz and Blackbird password. A suspect description alone is never promoted to a named murderer. Existing separate Trinsic research continues to distinguish killers, victims, Klog's claimed alibi and disputed Inamo motive.
- Fellowship: sealed package to Elynor in Minoc and the guide's Destard funds trap. No ordering prerequisite is asserted between the service and errand.
- Deep Forest: silent wisp search, Bee Cave honey as the emps' inducement, emps' whistle, communicating with the wisp and the requested notebook. Honey, whistle and notebook are distinct items with distinct purposes.
- Alagner/Skara Brae: answers request, Horance, potion, Soul Cage, Well of Souls, Caine's subsequent revelation, return for the notebook and notebook exchange to contact the Time Lord. The guide does not provide the full potion recipe or Soul Cage maker/materials; these are not invented.
- Generators: Deceit/Tetrahedron, Penumbra's blackrock, Draxinusom's Ethereal Ring and enchantment; Nicodemus's Hourglass enchantment explicitly after the Tetrahedron is down; Despise's down/up/down/down/up puzzle; northeast-sea meteorite Caddellite, Zorn in Minoc and worn helmets for the Cube. A single explicit after-condition is not a complete prerequisite graph.
- Endgame: Cube prism reveals Fellowship lies; Hook and the key in Buccaneer's Den; Isle of the Avatar/Hythloth; three prisms on pedestals disable the beam; Rudyom's Wand destroys the Black Gate. Searching for Hook and obtaining a key is kept separate from a claim that a particular key is already held.

## Forge of Virtue coverage

The manifest indexes Lord British's tremor conversation, Golden Ankh deed and magic crystal, Vesper departure and northern cove landing. Erethian's west-wing background, statues and individual test entrances are explicit access points.

Truth's true locket is reached before the labyrinth through the hood/north secret-wall route. Optional puzzle loot stays optional. Love's Stone of Castambre ritual is performed twice: revive Adjhar first, then give Bollux a heart, obtaining the Talisman of Love. These actions do not imply an invented family relationship. Courage's sleep-potion advice, body loot, helmet pedestal puzzle and Dracothraxus/Ether Gem are indexed; gaining the gem does not finish the later return phase. Museum lenses and Talismans of Principle go on the Dark Core before reporting to Lord British.

The Forge stat reward keeps the guide's **30, or 60 in some versions** qualification. It does not diagnose which value applies to a visitor's edition. The Castle of Fire door bug cause, version applicability and cheat recovery remain quarantined.

## Uncertainty and remaining gaps

The scope is **Ultima VII: The Black Gate plus the guide-authored Forge of Virtue**. There is no Serpent Isle knowledge, complete NPC dialogue transcript, exhaustive spell/reagent formula catalog, unsourced family tree, enemy numeric damage formula, undocumented immunity or platform-specific walkthrough.

The following boundaries are material:

- Seance speaks with Skara Brae spirits. The DB's travel claim is conflicted and is not asserted.
- Fellowship service/errand order, poison chest/key route and Castle door bug causation stay unresolved.
- Intended actions and conditional consequences are not evidence of actual death, completion, inventory, party membership or current location.
- Co-occurrence does not establish a motive, perpetrator, relative, inverse relationship or missing causal step.
- Existing outside research can answer its specifically reviewed facets only; it does not authorize uncited broader biographies.
- Many guide passages summarize a chain without the full mechanics. Potion ingredients, exact ritual interaction order beyond the source, precise routes within some dungeons and omitted minor quests remain genuine knowledge gaps.
