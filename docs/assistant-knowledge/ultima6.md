## Confidence and limits

The guide's own facts are the authoritative scope here. Existing separate outside-research entries remain explicitly labeled and cited by their original source metadata; this expansion adds none. Facts remain attributable to the guide and are not presented as independently play-tested mechanics.

The nine spell entries contain summarized circles/effects. They do not supply a comprehensive reagent, spell price, vendor, immunity or duration catalog. Quest instructions mention INVISIBILITY and PICKPOCKET; that does not prove these are identical to the spell-table entry Invisibility All. Equipment statistics do not support unsupplied combat formulas or optimal build rankings. The companion role text is the guide author's assessment. Cross-game biographies, undocumented family ties, Gargish etymologies, platform-specific behavior and unrecorded endings remain unsupported.

No edge is inferred from mere name co-occurrence. A quest's checkbox ordering is not a prerequisite graph. Source requirements may be explained, but possessing items, completing actions, joining parties and obtaining permission require actual supplied session/progress evidence. The assistant reads existing progress and never writes guide storage. An unknown newer subject must invalidate contextual reuse rather than silently returning an older answer.
