## Reviewed relationships

The Shadowlord groups are kept separate: Faulinei/Falsehood/Truth/Lycaeum; Astaroth/Hatred/Love/Empath Abbey; Nosfentor/Cowardice/Courage/Serpent's Hold. Each source instructs standing one tile south, shouting the correct name, waiting one turn and using the matching shard when that Shadowlord stands on the flame. The Nosfentor shard route does not explicitly say “take the shard” in its approach step; this expansion preserves its Hythloth/IN POR/Carpet route without inventing a pickup action.

FALLAX comes from Malifora after Malik's lead. The Hatred approach offers two entry routes: Felespar/MALUM/DAWN for Wrong or Rew's lead to Fiona/AVIDUS/DAWN for Covetous. Kaiko's reference lets Hassad teach IGNAVUS. Woolfe leads to Sindar for INFAMA. These explicit teachers, passwords and alternatives are recorded; a Resistance keyword is never substituted for a mantra or dungeon Word.

Lord Michael provides the Grapple, lives at Empath Abbey and follows the recorded day/bedroom schedule. The source states the hook should stay in active inventory. Stones at the harpsichord opens access to the Sandalwood Box; the same tune does not unlock every sealed room. The Carpet crosses the listed terrain, requires a Grapple for mountains and leaves the party vulnerable to rough sea. HMS Cape plans must be applied once to an obtained frigate; acquiring the plans alone is not an applied upgrade.

Sceptre acquisition explicitly calls for banishing Shadowlords first to prevent later reclamation. The Sceptre dispels fields; in Doom it dispels ethereal walls. The Crown protects when worn. The Amulet reveals Doom's hidden entrance; speaking VERAMOCOR is a separate entry action. The Box is given to Lord British through the final mirror, and the following source step says to escape with the Orb he releases. The Orb-giver edge cites both the named mirror step and its explicit pronoun continuation. No transitive dependency is inferred from artifacts merely appearing in one inventory list.

Gwenno is explicitly Iolo's wife. Saduj is explicitly Blackthorn's spy/agent. Their relationship facts do not assert either is currently in the visitor's party. David gives the free Sextant and Seggallion gives the Spyglass. The shrine-to-Codex referral, all-eight-shrines Word reward and Spirituality's active buried-moonstone gate at exactly midnight are stated conditions. Completing each shrine loop before the next is a recommendation: the source explicitly allows several pending shrine quests and warns that the Codex may resolve a different one.
