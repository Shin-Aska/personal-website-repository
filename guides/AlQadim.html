Player field manual

Fight like a Corsair

You begin as a second-level seafaring warrior: lightly equipped, quick on your feet, and already skilled with the scimitar. Survival is less about a hidden stat sheet and more about facing, timing, spacing, and choosing the right ranged tool.

The combat rhythm

Read the circles. Set your facing. Commit.

Combat is real time. The three action circles appear in dangerous areas and show which sword swings are currently available.

01

Face the threat

Your scimitar strikes in the direction you face. Press Alt to lock that direction in a corridor; unlock when surrounded.

02

Choose the swing

Click for the quick attack. Hold the action button longer and release when the second or third circle lights orange.

03

Respect recovery

Every blow has a brief readying pause. Land a clean hit, make space, then begin the next attack before closing again.

04

Switch the left hand

Ready either the seeking sling or one magic shard. Fire with Space in the direction your Corsair last faced.

Hu’rakan’s field notes

Win the exchange, not the spectacle

  • Charge before contact.Do not wait until you are beside a creature to ready the swing.
  • Hit and withdraw.Against powerful foes, secure one blow and back away before the return strike.
  • Keep moving.Slower creatures can be dodged; your moving feet make you harder to hit.
  • Use the pause.Opening the menu pauses combat, giving you time to drink a healing potion before the health bar empties.

♟️ Island of Senat Diplomatic Gauntlet

The Genie Lords negotiation sequence is complex and requires traveling across the chessboard grid to complete quests in the correct order.

Diplomacy Step-by-Step Flowchart

  1. Talk to the Djinn Lords (East) → they tell you to seek the Marid Lord.
  2. Talk to the Marid Lord (North) → he requests you free his Storyteller.
  3. Talk to the Dao Lord (West) → he requires the Pale Blue Jewel of Fire to release her.
  4. Return to the Djinn Lords → accept their terms, then say you will sail to the end of the world for Ardonat Isle. They grant the Jewel for your courage.
  5. Trade the Jewel to the Dao Lord → retrieve the Storyteller and escort her to the Marid Lord.
  6. Return her voice → the Marid Lord rewards you with the Efreet Potion.
  7. Drink the potion → cross the South chessboard path to the Efreet Lord.
