# Structured source-field index

`buildSourceFieldIndex(knowledge, {canonicalize})` returns `{entries, issues}`. Each entry includes `factId`, `recordId`, `sourceRecordId`, a compact `key`, generic `selectors`, a literal `witness`, canonical `subjectEntityIds`, optional `phase`, and exact reviewed `source` provenance. A raw structured entry has `factId: null`, `origin: "structured-source-record"`, and the full authorized `record`.

The index does not answer questions or follow graph edges. Rendering must use the complete fact or complete raw record. A selector witness span identifies coverage and cannot replace the condition-bearing complete answer. Source, edition, spoiler, query modifiers, current-state guards and atomic compound coverage remain caller responsibilities.

The six unchanged fact collections contain 674 documents. Metadata annotates 179 existing facts with 433 source fields. Together with narrow basic facet inference and 258 raw structured-record fields, the compiler emits 1143 entries, with zero compiler issues on the source fixtures. All 674 frozen document strings still match their existing vectors. Protected fact fields remain unchanged: `id`, `subject`, `facet`, `aliases`, `text`, `witness`, `source`, and `sourceRecordId`.

Named stats, role/focus/type and equipment coordinates come from exact structured provenance plus explicit typed labels. Tseramed's original location/profile/four-stat records are indexed directly, without creating a new fact or document. Narrative role fields were separately read from complete source witnesses: e.g. Ben supplies the yew log, Julia crafts panpipes, Arbeth spins thread, Charlotte weaves cloth, a broken lens is delivered to Lor-wis-lem, and Ephemerides receives a glass sword for the blue lens. Asking a person about an item does not prove a giver. An escort destination does not become a recipient. Objectives do not acquire procedure fields merely because they are requested as methods.

Three added source-container owners cover the party, balloon and Hawkins map source tasks. Each is explicitly declared, structurally shares the original parent, and has a literal parent label. These are retrieval/coverage scopes, not physical containment, ownership, progress or a relation inferred through a graph path.

Local fields require the entire fact witness in its exact authorized backing record and matching source-file lineage. External research fields retain existing dated local research witnesses/references; no upstream pages were fetched. Conflicting facts or backing records confer no fields. Authored owners must be the fact owner, a literal reviewed binding or its proved source-container owner. Invalid metadata is withheld atomically for that fact. Basic inference grants no named selector, construction material, participant relationship or phase.
