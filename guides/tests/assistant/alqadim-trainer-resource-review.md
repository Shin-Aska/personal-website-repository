## Shared resource-condition proof helper

The separately owned `resource-condition-requests.js` implements `parseResourceConditionRequest(question)` and `supportsResourceConditionRequest(descriptor, { fact, subjectEntityId, subjectLabels, grounded })`. Its finite descriptors preserve the complete explicit or pronominal subject, firing action, forever duration and suffix, or the resource, hit trigger and carrying alternative. It returns only a descriptor or boolean proof; it generates no answer text and resolves no entity or player state.

Resource-limit proof requires the complete literal per-fragment charges sentence and the same source's adjacent one-shot/one-charge plus empty-item destruction clause, with a matching canonical item or reviewed binding and a limitations role. A mere charge mention, firing instruction, missing binding, wrong item's depletion, or truncated witness fails. Trigger-contrast proof requires a literal affirmative `Drains Mana on Hit` effect scoped to the selected entity and an appropriate effect role. It permits quoting that recorded trigger only; it does not establish exclusivity, a negative effect from carrying, or a yes/no answer to the alternatives. The caller must retain the full source quote and common restrictions.
